# Product direction and competitive acceptance

Utsava should make the next useful action obvious. A broad feature menu alone does not establish market superiority. Validate task completion, return use and organizer effort with real households and organizations.

## Guided planning contract

1. Start with a name and occasion. Date, location and guest count stay optional.
2. Show one recommended action and at most three alternatives based on current authorized records.
3. Explain why an action matters and what information it needs. Let the user skip it.
4. Offer editable prompts such as “Help me choose the next three preparations” or “Compare these catering quotes using price, availability and dietary needs.”
5. Keep model suggestions separate from saved plan changes. Preview proposed edits and apply through existing authorized, version-checked server operations.
6. Never infer vendor confirmation, send messages, expose private measurements or initiate payments from generated text.
7. If AI is unavailable, retain a deterministic next-step list and explain that AI suggestions could not be generated.

## Benchmark matrix

| Market baseline | Utsava delivery target | Validation |
|---|---|---|
| Cvent: registration and attendee/event operations | Optional RSVP, registration, seating, check-in, schedule and reporting | A host runs an event-day flow without entering the same attendee twice |
| Cvent: venue sourcing and event coordination | Compare vendor availability, quotes, requirements and delivery states | A selected vendor is visibly distinct from a confirmed booking |
| HoneyBook: client portal visibility | Restricted guest/vendor workspace with explicit sharing | Vendor cannot access family records, budgets or another service's files |
| HoneyBook: projects, contacts and communication | Reusable planning structures and reliable communications | Reuse clears private records; delivery status reflects provider feedback |
| Utsava target differentiation | Family continuity, company-to-family transition, cultural templates, structured preparation | Parent hosts a private birthday while remaining a company participant |
| Utsava target differentiation | Next-action guidance with editable AI prompts | Users identify and complete their next task with fewer navigation steps |

This is an initial competitive baseline, not an exhaustive market audit or a claim that Utsava already exceeds these products.

## Proposed pilot metrics (not established outcomes)

- Time from sign-in to a persistent name-only draft.
- Time and number of navigation steps to finish the next preparation.
- Successful RSVP completion on mobile without installing an app.
- Percentage of hosts returning to create another occasion.
- Vendor booking mistakes and accidental sharing incidents.
- Birthday, wedding and Arangetram completion rates with and without guidance.
