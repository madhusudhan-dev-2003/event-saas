UTSAVA / REQUIREMENTS BASELINE / 01
One home for every celebration
Locked project requirements | Version 1.0 | September 13, 2026
This document consolidates the requirements approved in the project conversation and the saved
implementation plan. It is the target product specification, not a claim that all functionality has been
delivered.
Product decision
Continue building Utsava. Evolve the existing application into a general celebration planner for
individuals, families, academies, companies and communities. Preserve useful existing features and
records while replacing academy-only assumptions with optional modules.
Product promise
Help people organize their next celebration with less effort: a short setup, understandable
navigation, shared responsibilities, flexible templates and plans they can reuse. Broad household
adoption and competitive differentiation are ambitions, not guarantees of market exclusivity or zero
defects.

Scope boundaries
The software must support any number of events per space. Company participation and personal
hosting coexist under one login. Billing prices, storage limits and detailed commercial entitlements
have not been locked. Stripe is the selected subscription payment provider.

UTSAVA / REQUIREMENTS BASELINE / 02
Accounts, ownership and access
ACC-01 Use one identity/login across multiple independent spaces: Personal, Family and
Company/Academy. A person can belong to multiple organizations and retain their own family
space.
ACC-02 Each event belongs to exactly one owning space. Space membership and event-specific
participation define access. Invitations do not transfer event ownership or reveal a member's other
spaces.
ACC-03 A parent in a company event may also be a teacher elsewhere, a guest in another event
and a host in their own family space. Parent, teacher and host are contextual roles, not mutually
exclusive account types.
ACC-04 Company admins manage all authorized company records and functions. They do not
automatically see personal or family events. Platform support access is separate and audited.
ACC-05 A parent may buy a personal/family hosting plan without changing company permissions.
Invited company participation must not require that personal purchase. Leaving a company must not
remove personal events.
Role
Target scope
Space owner
Memberships, space settings, subscription and events in that space.
Company administrator
Company events, staff, student/parent/teacher assignments and organization
records.
Event host/cohost
Manage the particular event and its collaborators.
Teacher/coordinator
Assigned participants, schedules, rehearsals and tasks.
Parent/guardian
Explicitly linked children and permitted event responsibilities.
Student/participant
Permitted personal schedule and tasks; profile can exist without a login.
Vendor / guest
Only explicitly shared service or guest-facing information.
ACC-06 Invitation acceptance connects an existing identity rather than creating duplicate accounts.
Revoked or view-only members cannot edit. Guardian links and family membership require explicit
association; do not infer them from names or email domains.

UTSAVA / REQUIREMENTS BASELINE / 03
User workflows
Company onboarding and participation
1. Create company space and appoint its owner/admin.
 Next
2. Add student/participant profiles; invite teachers, parents and coordinators.
 Next
3. Recipients sign in or complete onboarding and explicitly accept access.
 Next
4. Create events and assign people, responsibilities and relevant permissions.
Parent becomes a personal host
1. Participate in the company event using the same login.
 Next
2. Choose Create my own event and select/create a personal or family space.
 Next
3. Choose an occasion, name a draft and customize the suggested plan.
 Next
4. Upgrade the family space when paid hosting features are needed. Company access stays
separate.
Event lifecycle
Choose occasion > Name draft > Personalize > Invite and assign > Track preparations >
Event-day checklist > Photos and thank-yous > Archive or reuse.
Guest and vendor journeys
Guest: restricted invitation > household RSVP > permitted event details. A guest should not need
an app installation or planner access.
Vendor: requirements > compare options > family selects > booking confirmed > delivery tracked >
payment recorded. Selection alone does not confirm a booking.

UTSAVA / REQUIREMENTS BASELINE / 04
Templates for every occasion
TMP-01 Provide the occasion starters below and a blank start. Templates configure one shared
event system; they are not separate applications.
Occasion
Relevant starting content
Birthday
Guests, cake, food, activities, gifts and photos.
Wedding
Multiple functions, household RSVP, seating, ceremonies, vendors and outfits.
Graduation
Honorees, invitations, program, catering and photography.
Halloween
Theme, costumes, treats, activities and potluck.
Thanksgiving
Meal contributions, dietary needs and seating.
Christmas
Gathering schedule, food and optional gift exchange.
Diwali
Food, decor, optional ceremonies and performances.
Arangetram
Students, gurus, rehearsals, program, costumes, jewellery, makeup and orchestra.
Get-together
Date planning/poll, invitations and food contributions.
Company celebration
Teams, coordinators, approvals, budget, registration and vendors.
TMP-02 Template content includes suggested tasks, relative deadlines, budget categories, service
categories, applicable forms, invitation theme and optional modules. It must not insert real/example
personal data or assume a booked vendor.
TMP-03 Users can remove sections, add custom fields, rename relevant labels and save reusable
personal templates. Store a versioned event snapshot; later template changes must not overwrite
customizations.
TMP-04 Show student/guru/training fields only when relevant. A birthday must not require them.
Support one parent event with multiple functions, each with its own time, venue, guest selection and
schedule.
TMP-05 On date changes, propose changes to relative deadlines while preserving explicitly fixed
deadlines. Reuse structures safely; do not silently copy prior guests, payments, private documents,
measurements or vendor commitments.

UTSAVA / REQUIREMENTS BASELINE / 05
People, vendors and preparation
PPL-01 Maintain student/participant profiles independently of login accounts. Support multiple
children per parent, explicit guardian permissions and teacher assignment, including adding a new
teacher.
PPL-02 Keep identity details, training, family relationships, season/event assignments and account
access understandable. Collect only fields relevant to the current step and action.
VEN-01 Maintain vendor profiles, service categories, contacts, availability, quotations and event
assignments. Offer multiple providers for every service and an option to use the family's own
vendor. Families choose the provider they prefer; company admins manage company
arrangements.
VEN-02 Keep shortlisted, selected, confirmed, delivered/completed and paid states distinct.
Compare prices, availability and service details without implying that a preference is a contract or
payment.
Service
Required capability; individual values optional until needed
Tailoring / dress
Structured measurement form with units (inches/centimeters), dress color, fabric,
design details/reference attachments, fitting dates, delivery dates and notes.
Jewellery
Design references and details for short haram, long haram, bangles, hair set, waist
belt and earrings; support additional components and rental/purchase, collection and
return details.
Makeup
Overall look/colors; pancake or foundation brand and shade; lipstick, eyeliner,
eyeshadow, blush, hair/flowers, product preferences, appointment details and
reference designs.
Other services
Catering/menu/headcount/dietary needs; photography coverage and deliverables;
venue, decor, audio, orchestra and custom service requirements.
VEN-03 Allow independent vendors for tailoring, jewellery and makeup. Preserve requirements per
participant/event when applicable. Private measurements, files and contacts must only be shared
with explicitly authorized recipients.
VEN-04 Provide usable preparation sheets for coordination, including print/export where applicable.
Saving requirements does not automatically complete a fitting, confirm a booking, send an invitation
or transfer money.

UTSAVA / REQUIREMENTS BASELINE / 06
Core planning and payments
MOD-01 Overview: show what needs attention, what happens next, who is responsible and what is
waiting for confirmation. Summaries must use real records, not demo values.
MOD-02 Guests: household invitations and RSVP, guest counts, dietary needs, appropriate guest
visibility, seating and event-day check-in when enabled.
MOD-03 Plan: tasks, owners/contributors, deadlines, schedule, dependencies and event-day
checklist. Support reminders and announcements with reliable delivery status.
MOD-04 Budget: planned, committed and paid amounts; expenses, obligations, invoices,
contributions, approvals and reports as relevant. Preserve currency clarity and payment status.
MOD-05 Files: design references, preparation documents and private attachments with controlled
access. Optional food contributions, seating, functions, rehearsals and ticketing appear only when
enabled.
MOD-06 Preserve the useful academy modules: seasons, events, timeline/tasks, students, vendors,
guests, budget/payments, reports, user management, access control and profile/settings. Imports,
exports, draft resumption and reports must work accurately when offered.
Three separate money flows
Payment
Purpose and ownership
Utsava subscription
Software capabilities for the purchasing personal/family/company space.
Stripe selected.
Organizer/event fee
Participation fee or contribution paid to the event organizer.
Vendor payment
Money associated with a particular booked service.
BIL-01 Buying a family subscription must not grant extra company permissions. Paying an academy
fee must not be mistaken for purchasing a software subscription.
BIL-02 Activate entitlements only after verified provider confirmation. Handle duplicate/retried
webhooks, payment failures, renewal and cancellation. Cancellation must not delete existing event
data.
BIL-03 Personal/family and company hosting plans are in scope. Exact prices, commercial feature
limits, trials and any single-event purchase option remain decisions to finalize; do not invent or
advertise them as approved.

UTSAVA / REQUIREMENTS BASELINE / 07
Navigation and experience
UX-01 Global hierarchy: My celebrations > Space > Event. Always make the current space and
event clear. Users must understand where their data belongs and how to return to the event list.
UX-02 Use consistent event navigation: Overview / Plan / Guests / Budget / Vendors / Settings.
Place enabled specialized modules predictably without overwhelming a simple celebration.
UX-03 Create a draft with an event name only. Date, time, location, budget and guest count can be
supplied later. Require information when an action genuinely needs it, with a brief explanation.
UX-04 Use readable, generous typography and clearly visible input boxes. Outfit is the selected font
trial; preserve the ability to revert. Keep the maroon/gold/ivory identity with a professional,
celebration-friendly appearance.
UX-05 Apply uniform spacing, forms, cards, labels, buttons and feedback across student, vendor,
event and other pages. Themes may alter event artwork/accents while planning controls remain
familiar. Use Figma for design work where appropriate.
UX-06 Keep one clear primary action per form. Provide pending, success and error feedback; label
optional fields; preserve entered values on errors; warn before losing unsaved changes. Buttons
must perform the action their labels promise.
UX-07 Support mobile layouts, legible text, keyboard navigation, visible focus, meaningful control
labels and usable dialogs. Essential actions must not depend on hover.
UX-08 Support upcoming, draft and past/archived events, useful search/filtering and reuse. Empty
states should explain the next useful action. Hide unavailable controls or explain their state; never
simulate a successful action.
Differentiation to pursue
Family continuity across occasions; easy company-to-family transition; culturally flexible templates;
practical coordination of people, requirements and vendors; low-effort guest participation. Validate
these through real repeat use rather than assuming feature count proves superiority.

UTSAVA / REQUIREMENTS BASELINE / 08
Architecture and migration
ARC-01 Keep the existing Next.js/PostgreSQL application and develop Utsava's next version in
stages. Use clearly separated internal modules rather than introducing unnecessary microservices.
Layer
Responsibility
Experience
Responsive web interface, space/event context and consistent navigation.
Identity and access
Authenticated identity, memberships, event roles, guardian links and explicit
sharing.
Event domain
Events, functions, template versions, configuration snapshots and lifecycle.
Operations
Guests, tasks/schedule, budgets, services, files and communications.
Commercial
Space subscriptions, entitlements and provider integrations.
Infrastructure
PostgreSQL, private object storage, background jobs, notifications and audit
records.
ARC-02 Key relationships: User > Membership > Space; Space > Events; Event >
participants/tasks/guests/services/files; Person profile > guardian links; Template version > event
configuration; Space > subscription.
ARC-03 Authorize every read and write on the server. Never grant access from a URL role
parameter or global admin label alone. Restrict guest/vendor data and use the actual session
identity for user overrides.
ARC-04 Use transactional writes and foreign keys for ownership/relationships. Use version checks
to reject stale edits. Make retries safe so they do not silently duplicate records or overwrite another
user's work.
ARC-05 Preserve academy records and keep existing workflows available while the new foundation
is tested. Isolated celebration tables are a temporary migration boundary; consolidate through an
explicit adapter/migration, not a permanent pair of disconnected products.
ARC-06 Use the canonical local project at Documents/Projects/Utsav/event-software for edits, Git
and localhost. Save changes in version control and test a preview before production. The intended
production domain is event.rkldigital.com.
ARC-07 Deploy migrations with the correct production project and verify recovery/rollback behavior.
Do not treat a local build or Git push as proof of a successful production deployment.

UTSAVA / REQUIREMENTS BASELINE / 09
Acceptance and delivery status
Release acceptance criteria
QA-01 A parent can participate in a company event, host a private birthday, switch spaces and
retain company membership without exposing family data.
QA-02 Outsiders, revoked members and viewers cannot modify protected records. Altered IDs/role
URLs do not bypass checks. Guests and vendors see only permitted content.
QA-03 Name-only drafts survive refresh; malformed data is rejected; retries/concurrent edits do not
cause silent duplication or data loss; templates never overwrite customized events.
QA-04 Test complete birthday and Arangetram journeys, plus a complex wedding/cohost flow.
Confirm buttons, module links, filters, forms, imports, exports, relationships and status changes
using real persistence.
QA-05 Check desktop/mobile and keyboard flows, authorization regressions, production build,
database migration and external provider callbacks. A rendered page or success toast alone does
not constitute a pass.
Checkpoint - not a completion claim
Implemented locally: isolated spaces, occasion starters, name-only drafts, basic manual planning
sections, membership-checked saves, expiring invitation acceptance for existing verified accounts,
safe reuse and archive status. The latest audit repaired simulated permission saving and several
navigation defects.
Still incomplete: unified academy/family migration, event-specific roles and guardian integration,
invitation onboarding/member management, public RSVP/delivery, structured preparation
integration, private attachments, functions/seating/food, Stripe implementation/configuration and
production release.
Latest recorded checks: 261 tests passed; TypeScript and production compilation passed; lint had
0 errors and 117 warnings. Main admin routes rendered. This does not certify every role or every
workflow.
Decisions and dependencies still open
Stripe credentials and approved plan prices/price IDs; detailed hosting entitlements; storage and
delivery service configuration; actual production Vercel project access; complete migration and
cross-account acceptance testing. Do not send credentials in this requirements document.
